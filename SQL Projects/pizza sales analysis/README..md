🗄️ SQL Business Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data using **MySQL** to uncover business insights related to sales performance, profitability, products, customers, and trends.

The project demonstrates my ability to use SQL to explore data, answer business questions, and transform raw data into meaningful insights that can support business decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

A Pizza resturant wants to better understand its sales performance and customer purchasing behavior. Although the resturant has collected a large amount of sales transaction data, it needs a structured analysis to identify important trends, high-performing products, valuable customers, and areas where business performance can be improved.

The objective of this project is to use **SQL to explore and analyze the sales data** and answer important business questions related to revenue, profitability, products, customers, and sales trends.


The final goal is to transform the raw sales data into **meaningful business insights** that can support better decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis will answer the following questions:

KPI's REQUIREMENT

We need to analyze key indicators for our pizza sales data to gain insights into our business
performance. Specifically, we want to calculate the following metrics:

1. Total Revenue: The sum of the total price of all pizza orders.

2. Average Order Value: The average amount spent per order, calculated by dividing the
total revenue by the total number of orders.

3. Total Pizzas Sold: The sum of the quantities of all pizzas sold.

4. Total Orders: The total number of orders placed.

5. Average Pizzas Per Order: The average number of pizzas sold per order, calculated by
dividing the total number of pizzas sold by the total number of orders.

6.Daily Trend for Total Orders:
Create a bar chart that displays the daily trend of total orders over a specific time period. This chart will
help us identify any patterns or fluctuations in order volumes on a daily basis.

7.Hourly Trend for Total Orders:
Create a line chart that illustrates the hourly trend of total orders throughout the day. This chart will allow
us to identify peak hours or periods of high order activity.

8.Percentage of Sales by Pizza Category:
Create a pie chart that shows the distribution of sales across different pizza categories. This chart will
provide insights into the popularity of various pizza categories and their contribution to overall sales.

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Lower Order Volume on Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday

Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday have a lower number of orders compared with the other days of the week.

**Recommendation:**
The business could introduce special offers, discounts, or promotions on these days to encourage more customers to place orders and increase order volume during slower periods.

### 2. Classic Pizza Category Dominates Orders

The **Classic** pizza category has the highest number of orders, with a noticeable difference compared with the other categories.

**Recommendation:**
The business could introduce targeted offers or promotions for the other pizza categories to encourage customers to try a wider variety of products and increase sales across those categories.
