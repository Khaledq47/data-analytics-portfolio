# Plants Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing plant sales performance using Microsoft Power BI. The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards and data visualization.

The analysis explores overall sales performance, profitability, product segmentation, and geographical distribution to help understand business performance and identify potential growth opportunities.

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Evaluate overall sales and financial performance.
- Analyze sales and profit trends over time.
- Identify the highest-performing countries by sales.
- Understand sales distribution across product types and sizes.
- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Identify opportunities to improve profitability and business performance.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX, and interactive dashboards.
- **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and business metrics.
- **Data Visualization** – KPI cards, bar charts, column charts, and line charts.

## Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard results:

**1. Overall Financial Performance**

- Total sales reached 30.08M.
- Total profit reached 11.96M.
- The overall profit margin was 39.77%, indicating a positive profitability level.

**2. Sales Performance Over Time**

- Sales reached approximately 13.5M in 2022.
- Sales decreased slightly to 13.0M in 2023.
- Sales recorded approximately 3.6M in 2024.

The 2024 performance requires further investigation to determine whether the decline reflects actual business performance or incomplete yearly data.

**3. Geographical Performance**

China was the leading country, generating approximately 10.0M in sales, followed by:

- Brazil: 2.3M
- Philippines: 2.1M
- Portugal: 1.6M
- Poland: 1.5M

China represents a significant share of overall sales, highlighting the importance of this market to the business.

**4. Product Performance**

The dashboard compares sales across three product types:

- Indoor
- Landscape
- Outdoor

It also examines three product sizes: Large, Medium, and Small, helping identify differences in customer demand across product segments.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the initial findings, the following recommendations could help improve business performance:

1. **Investigate Sales Decline:** Examine the sales decrease in 2024 and identify whether it is related to seasonality, market conditions, or incomplete data.

2. **Diversify Geographical Markets:** Reduce dependency on the leading market by exploring opportunities in other countries.

3. **Optimize Product Portfolio:** Identify high-performing product types and sizes to support inventory and sales planning.

4. **Monitor Profitability:** Track profit margins across countries and products to identify the most profitable segments.

5. **Improve Sales Strategy:** Use historical sales trends to support forecasting, promotional planning, and business decisions.

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates the use of Power BI to transform sales data into an interactive business intelligence solution.

It combines data preparation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, and data visualization to provide a clearer understanding of sales performance and support data-driven business decisions.
